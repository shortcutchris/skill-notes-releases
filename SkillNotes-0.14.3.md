# Skill Notes 0.14.3

Formatting in Card View now feels like formatting in a floating sticky note.

- Select text to show the shared formatting bar directly beside the selection.
- Use Aa to open the same bar at the current cursor.
- Keep typing, press Return, or copy text without losing editor focus or
  accidentally activating a formatting control.
- Dismiss the bar predictably with Escape, scrolling, app deactivation, or a
  click outside the editor.

The underlying note remains canonical Markdown, so card and floating-window
edits continue to share autosave, undo, tasks, revisions, and iCloud sync.
